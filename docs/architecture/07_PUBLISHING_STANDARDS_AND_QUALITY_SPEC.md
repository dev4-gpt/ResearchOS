# Layer Specification: Journal Publishing Standards, Page/Word Thresholds & Writing Specification

## 1. Overview & Purpose

This specification establishes the official academic publishing guidelines, quantitative page/word count thresholds, typographic formatting rules, and voice/style requirements for manuscripts generated by **ResearchingOS**.

To ensure acceptance in top-tier computer science, AI, and management information systems venues (**IEEE TKDE, ACM CSUR, Nature Machine Intelligence, MIS Quarterly, NeurIPS/ICML Survey Tracks**), ResearchingOS strictly enforces senior principal-level rigor, zero commercial hype, and comprehensive length requirements.

## 2. Quantitative Manuscript Thresholds

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│               QUANTITATIVE MANUSCRIPT LENGTH THRESHOLDS                                │
├───────────────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────┬────────────────────────┤
│ Manuscript Category           │ Minimum Page Count (IEEE 2-Col)│ Minimum Word Count     │
├───────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────┤
│ Systematic Literature Reviews │ 15 to 25+ Pages               │ 8,000 to 15,000+ Words │
│ Full Empirical Research Papers│ 10 to 14 Pages                │ 6,000 to 9,500 Words   │
│ Short Survey Letters / Briefs │ 6 to 8 Pages                  │ 3,500 to 5,000 Words   │
└───────────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────┴────────────────────────┘
```

- **Mandatory Enforcement**: The `Senior Research Writer` agent and `LaTeXExporterService` must never produce brief summary notes under 15 pages for full systematic review tasks.
- **Section Word Allocation**:
  - Executive Abstract & Index Terms: 300 – 400 words
  - Section 1 (Introduction & Paradigm Shift): 1,200 – 1,500 words
  - Section 2 (PRISMA 2020 Search & Methodologies): 1,000 – 1,200 words
  - Section 3 (5-Pillar Meta-Taxonomy): 2,500 – 3,500 words
  - Section 4 (Empirical Meta-Analysis Matrix & Tables): 1,000 – 1,500 words
  - Section 5 (Systems Architecture & FLOPs/KV-Cache Math): 1,200 – 1,500 words
  - Section 6 (Hostile Peer Review / Reviewer #2 Audit): 1,000 – 1,200 words
  - Section 7 (Strategic Enterprise Roadmap): 800 – 1,000 words
  - Section 8 (Conclusion & References): 500 – 800 words

## 3. Typographic & LaTeX Formatting Standards

### 3.1 IEEEtran Two-Column Format
- **Document Class**: `\documentclass[10pt,journal,compsoc,twocolumn]{IEEEtran}`
- **Font Specifications**:
  - Body Text: **10pt Computer Modern / Times New Roman** serif, single-spaced two-column.
  - Section Headings: **10pt Bold Uppercase** (e.g. `\section{1. INTRODUCTION}`).
  - Subsection Headings: **10pt Italic** (e.g. `\subsection{2.1 Empirical Productivity}`).
  - Table & Figure Captions: **8pt Small Caps / Serif**.
- **Page Geometry & Margins**:
  - Top/Bottom Margins: **0.75 in (19.1 mm)**
  - Left/Right Margins: **0.625 in (15.9 mm)**
  - Column Separation: **0.16 in (4.1 mm)**

### 3.2 Color Palette & Graphical Elements
- **Monochrome & High-Contrast Visuals**: Use clean grayscale and muted indigo/emerald accents for diagrams (`#3b82f6` primary, `#10b981` success, `#8b5cf6` violet).
- **Mermaid & Vector Diagrams**: Flowcharts and system graphs must render with explicit node labels and zero HTML tags inside labels.

## 4. Senior Writer Tone & Anti-Hype Directives

### 4.1 Persona & Voice Rules
- **Identity**: Senior Principal Researcher and 20-year academic institute director.
- **Tone**: Formal, objective, analytical, quantitative, and authoritative.
- **Prohibited Commercial Hype Words**:
  - ❌ Never use buzzwords: *"game-changer"*, *"revolutionary"*, *"groundbreaking"*, *"delve"*, *"paradigm-shifting"*, *"testament"*, *"seamless"*.
  - ✅ Use precise academic verbs: *"demonstrates"*, *"establishes"*, *"exposes"*, *"quantifies"*, *"formulates"*, *"delineates"*.

### 4.2 Mathematical & Rigor Standards
- **Formal Equations**: Every technical section must include explicit mathematical equations (e.g., inference compute scaling laws $\mathcal{C}_{\text{pipeline}}$, KV-cache memory footprints, statistical $p$-values, confidence intervals).
- **Zero Invented Claims**: Every numeric claim ($N=...$, $\%$, $p$-values) must be cross-referenced against ingested vault paper notes (`vault/01_Papers/`).
- **Obsidian Wikilink Citations**: Every paper reference must be cited inline as `[[paper_id]]` matching exact vault filenames.
