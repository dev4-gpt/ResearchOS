# O-1A Academic Publisher & Venue Alignment Skill

Use this skill when drafting, compiling, auditing, or exporting research manuscripts for top-tier artificial intelligence conferences (NeurIPS, ICML, CVPR, ACL) and journals (IEEEtran, ACM).

---

## 1. Technical Specifications & O-1A Criteria Mapping

### 1. NeurIPS (Neural Information Processing Systems)
- **Formatting**: Single-column layout, 10pt Times font. Max 9 pages (main content) + unlimited refs.
- **Anonymization**: Double-blind. Mask all author names, Penn State institutional affiliations, and grant IDs (`Anonymous Project GitHub`).
- **O-1A Criterion**: **8 CFR § 204.5(h)(3)(vi) [Scholarly Articles]**. Highlight sub-15% oral / sub-25% poster acceptance rates in petition letters.
- **Citation Multiplier**: Open-source training algorithms via Hugging Face & GitHub to maximize citation velocity.

### 2. ICML (International Conference on Machine Learning)
- **Formatting**: Two-column layout, US Letter size. Max 9 pages for camera-ready.
- **Anonymization**: Double-blind. Use third-person self-citations ("Smith et al. [1] previously proved...").
- **O-1A Criterion**: **8 CFR § 204.5(h)(3)(v) [Original Contributions of Major Significance]**. Focus on core optimization stability or architectural efficiency.
- **Citation Multiplier**: Release an executable benchmarking suite or dataset (3x higher citation loops).

### 3. CVPR (Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition)
- **Formatting**: Two-column layout, line numbers enabled for review draft. 8 pages max + refs.
- **Anonymization**: Strict anonymization. Strip camera EXIF data and sensor metadata from supplemental assets.
- **O-1A Criterion**: **8 CFR § 204.5(h)(3)(iii) [Published Material About Alien / Media Coverage]**. Ideal for public and technical media coverage.
- **Citation Multiplier**: Host an interactive Gradio demo on Hugging Face Spaces.

### 4. ACL (Association for Computational Linguistics)
- **Formatting**: Two-column layout, ACL Rolling Review (ARR) style. 8 pages (long paper) / 4 pages (short paper).
- **Anonymization**: Mask model weights, model hosting links, and fine-tuning prompt templates.
- **O-1A Criterion**: **8 CFR § 204.5(h)(3)(iv) [Judging the Work of Others]**. Fast-tracks program committee and workshop reviewer invitations.
- **Citation Multiplier**: Publish complete prompt libraries and system messages in structured repositories.

### 5. IEEEtran & ACM Computing Surveys (Journal Reviews)
- **Formatting**: Two-column journal format. 10 to 25+ pages for systematic literature reviews.
- **O-1A Criterion**: **8 CFR § 204.5(h)(3)(vi) [Scholarly Articles in Major Journals]**. High-density, zero-slop systematic review matrices.

## 2. High-Velocity PME (Problem-Method-Experiment) Structural Mandate

All manuscript introductions MUST follow the strict **Problem-Method-Experiment (PME)** paradigm:
1. **Problem**: Clear statement of enterprise engineering bottlenecks, unmetered compute scaling, or epistemological evaluation circularity.
2. **Method**: 5-Pillar Meta-Taxonomy, mathematical FLOPs/KV-cache scaling equations, or multi-agent orchestration frameworks.
3. **Experiment**: PRISMA 2020 protocol, $N \ge 1,000$ sample sizes, 95% Clopper-Pearson confidence intervals, and compute-equivalent baseline controls.

---

## 3. O-1A Evidence Tracking Rules

Always audit manuscripts against the 4 key O-1A legal criteria:
1. **Scholarly Articles** (8 CFR § 204.5(h)(3)(vi))
2. **Original Scientific Contributions of Major Significance** (8 CFR § 204.5(h)(3)(v))
3. **Judging the Work of Others** (8 CFR § 204.5(h)(3)(iv))
4. **Published Material About the Alien** (8 CFR § 204.5(h)(3)(iii))
