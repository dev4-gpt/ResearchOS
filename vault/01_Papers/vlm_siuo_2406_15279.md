The abstract defines SIUO cases in which modalities appear safe independently but their combination can produce unsafe output, and identifies vulnerabilities across tested open and closed LVLMs. It is a related interaction benchmark. Its compositional-risk question differs from the current typographic rendering gap and from a fixed intervention's text/image safety trade-off.

Primary source: [arXiv version 2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.15279v2). The arXiv record lists NAACL 2025; full proceedings and methodological details remain to be inspected.
