## Core Claims & Hypotheses

1. **The Novelty-Quality Trade-Off:** Solutions generated solely by the Human Crowd (HC) exhibit higher average and extreme-value [[Novelty]] compared to solutions generated through [[Human-AI Collaboration]].
2. **Human-AI Dominance on Pragmatic Dimensions:** Human-AI co-created solutions outperform pure human crowd solutions in [[Strategic Viability]], [[Financial Value]], [[Environmental Value]], and [[Overall Quality]].
3. **Search Paradigm Superiority:** Human-AI solutions generated via **Differentiated Search** (sequential generation instructed to produce outputs distinct from previous iterations) outperform those generated via **Independent Search**.

## Study Design & Experimental Methodology

The study implemented a crowdsourcing challenge focused on generating sustainable, circular economy business ideas. It contrasted pure human ideation with human-guided generative AI ideation workflows.

```
                  ┌────────────────────────────────────────┐
                  │ 125 Global Solvers (Circular Economy)  │
                  └───────────────────┬────────────────────┘
                                      │
             ┌────────────────────────┴────────────────────────┐
             ▼                                                 ▼
┌─────────────────────────┐                       ┌─────────────────────────┐
│   Human Crowd (HC)      │                       │     Human-AI Solvers     │
│   Pure Human Design     │                       │ (Strategic Prompting)   │
└─────────────────────────┘                       └────────────┬────────────┘
                                                               │
                                       ┌───────────────────────┴───────────────────────┐
                                       ▼                                               ▼
                         ┌───────────────────────────┐                   ┌───────────────────────────┐
                         │    Independent Search     │                   │   Differentiated Search   │
                         │   (Parallel Iterations)   │                   │ (Sequential, Divergent)   │
                         └───────────────────────────┘                   └───────────────────────────┘
```

### Participant and Solution Sample Sizes
- **Solvers ($N_{solvers}$):** 125 global solvers recruited from various industries.
- **Total Generated Solutions ($|S|$):** 234 solutions.
- **External Evaluators ($N_{evaluators}$):** 300 external human evaluators.

### Evaluation Protocol (Matrix Setup)
The evaluation was structured as a randomized, sparse rating block to minimize evaluator fatigue while maintaining statistical power:
- Each evaluator $i \in \{1, \dots, 300\}$ was randomly assigned a subset of solutions $S_i \subset S$.
- Cardinality of evaluation subset: $|S_i| = 13$.
- Total observed evaluator-solution pairs ($N_{observations}$):
  $$N_{observations} = \sum_{i=1}^{300} |S_i| = 300 \times 13 = 3,900 \text{ rating pairs}$$

## Human-AI Search Paradigms

The study compared two distinct prompt engineering search strategies with [[Large Language Models]]:

### 1. Independent Search
- **Definition:** The LLM generates solutions in isolation or parallel iterations without explicit memory-guided deviation instructions.
- **Mechanism:** Multiple parallel runs using standard prompts to explore the solution space independently.

### 2. Differentiated Search
- **Definition:** A sequential, human-guided prompting pipeline designed to enforce divergent evolution of ideas.
- **Mechanism:** The solver instructs the LLM to sequentially analyze its previous outputs and explicitly generate subsequent concepts that are structurally, operationally, or strategically distinct from prior generations.

| Evaluation Metric | Superior Cohort | Key Qualitative & Statistical Observations |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **[[Novelty]] (Average)** | **Human Crowd (HC)** | Pure human efforts consistently produced more novel baseline concepts. |

| **[[Strategic Viability]]** | **Human-AI** | Human-AI solutions demonstrated higher feasibility and alignment with real-world business constraints. |
| **[[Financial Value]]** | **Human-AI** | Human-AI solutions possessed more coherent and robust monetization models. |

| **[[Overall Quality]]** | **Human-AI** | Human-AI solutions scored higher on composite quality scales. |
| **Search Performance Comparison** | **Differentiated > Independent** | Within the AI group, [[Differentiated Search]] yielded significantly stronger results than [[Independent Search]]. |

## Methodological Limitations

- **Context Specificity:** The experimental sandbox was limited to sustainable, circular economy business concepts; results may differ in highly abstract, artistic, or purely technical/mathematical domains.
- **Technological Snapshot:** The performance of the "AI in the loop" model is bound to the capabilities of the specific LLM generation utilized at the time of the study (2023–2024 models).
- **Prompt Sensitivity:** The effectiveness of the Human-AI collaboration relies heavily on the solvers' ability to design and execute strategic prompt engineering workflows.
