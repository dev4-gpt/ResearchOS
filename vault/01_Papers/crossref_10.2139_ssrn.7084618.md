# Autonomous Governance of Agentic Financial Systems: A Multi-Agent Regulatory Framework for Real-Time Market Integrity Surveillance and Systemic Risk Mitigation

**Authors**: Prakhar Rai
**Published**: 2026-7-25 | **Citations**: 0 | **Source**: Crossref
**URL**: https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7084618

## Executive Summary & Abstract
The rapid deployment of Agentic AI systems in financial markets has outpaced existing governance frameworks, creating unprecedented systemic risks from emergent behaviors such as algorithmic collusion, spoofing, and cascading market destabilization. This paper proposes and implements an Auto-Regulatory Agentic Framework (ARAF) comprising specialized AI agents that autonomously monitor trading behavior, detect emergent anomalies, and execute real-time regulatory interventions. Using a simulated multi-agent market environment with heterogeneous trading strategies (normal, spoofing, collusive), we demonstrate that our framework achieves a detection rate of 90.3% for anomalous behaviors, reduces portfolio volatility by 32.1%, and decreases maximum drawdown by 41.5% compared to unregulated markets. The framework operationalizes the "Agentic Regulator" paradigm through practical implementation on resource-constrained environments (Google Colab), demonstrating that autonomous regulatory agents can effectively quarantine malicious trading entities while preserving market innovation. Our ablation study confirms the critical contribution of each regulatory component-behavioral anomaly detection, collusion monitoring, market stability assessment, and LLM-based reasoning-to overall governance effectiveness. This research provides the first empirical validation of agentic regulatory architectures for real-time financial market governance.
