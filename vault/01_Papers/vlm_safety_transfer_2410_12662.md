The abstract reports that text safety mechanisms may fail to transfer to vision because of hidden-state semantic shifts, and proposes text-guided alignment. This supports modality transfer as established prior work and motivates testing both transfer failure and transfer success. It does not assert a zero-sum law between text and image safety.
