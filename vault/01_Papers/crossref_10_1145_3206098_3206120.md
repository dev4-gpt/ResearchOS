# Complex Adaptive Systems

**Authors**: Saba Arshad, Hafsa Maryam, Humera Yasmeen, Munam Ali Shah
**Published**: 2018 | **Source**: Crossref
**URL**: https://doi.org/10.1145/3206098.3206120

## Abstract
Complex and Adaptive Systems (CAS) consists of large number of elements that are connected with each other and interact in distributed manner. CAS exhibits random behavior which cannot be pre determined and is a result of non-linear interaction of large number of elements. In CAS, the elements adapt to the changes in environment and system. Thus change in any single element can affect its neighboring elements which may lead to the vast change in the whole system. CAS has shown excellent potential in enhancing communication capabilities, fault tolerance, trust, energy efficiency, cascade failures avoidance and handling, and advanced miscellaneous new applications and services. In this research, we provide a combined review of different applications of CAS and categorized them as natural and artificial CAS. Also, the major CAS challenges and techniques proposed to overcome these challenges are disscused. The limitations of the existing literature are also identified. Lastly, this research sheds light on future directions. To the best of our knowledge, it is the first review paper which provides combined discussion about different applications and challenges of CAS and identifies different research dimensions for further research.
