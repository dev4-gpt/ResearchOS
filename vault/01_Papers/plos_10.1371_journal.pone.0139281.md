# [[Scott2015Viewpoints]]

## 1. Research Aim / Hypotheses

*   **Aim**: To explore the key factors for [[Successful Employment]] from the viewpoints of [[Adults with Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD)]] and [[Employers]].
*   **Claim**: Differences in understanding regarding workplace support, job expectations, and productivity hinder [[Successful Employment]] for [[Adults with Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD)]].

## 2. Methodology

*   **Approach**: [[Q method]] was utilized to understand and contrast the viewpoints of the two participant groups.
*   **Data Analysis**: Data were analyzed using [[by-person varimax rotation factor analysis]].

## 3. Participants / Datasets

*   **Sample Size (N)**:
    *   [[Adults with Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD)]]: 40 participants
    *   [[Employers]]: 35 participants

## 4. Key Findings / Results

*   Both [[Adults with Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD)]] and [[Employers]] appear committed to the [[Employment Process]].
*   A significant difference exists in the understanding between these two groups regarding:
    *   The type of [[Workplace Support]] required.
    *   [[Job Expectations]].
    *   [[Productivity Requirements]].
*   These differences continue to hinder [[Successful Employment]].

## 5. Limitations

*   No specific limitations were explicitly stated in the provided text.

## 6. Recommendations / Future Work

*   There is a need to facilitate [[Communication]] between [[Employees]] and [[Employers]] to ensure a clear understanding of the needs of both groups.
*   The development and use of an [[ASD-Specific Workplace Tool]] may assist in facilitating this necessary communication between [[Adults with Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD)]] and [[Employers]].
