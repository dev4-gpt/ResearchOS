# [[A Review of TRiSM Frameworks in Artificial Intelligence Systems]]

## Abstract Summary

The paper addresses the urgent need for unified [[Trust_Risk_Security_Management]] ([[TRiSM]]) frameworks within the [[AI_Lifecycle]], especially given the rapid expansion of [[Generative_AI]] and [[Large_Language_Models]] ([[LLMs]]) into mission-critical domains. It presents a comprehensive review and synthesis of [[AI_TRiSM]], structuring it around five foundational pillars: [[Explainability]] with [[Real-Time_Drift_Monitoring]], [[ModelOps_Governance]], [[Application-Level_Security]], [[Data_Protection_and_Privacy]], and [[Adversarial_Resilience]]. The work introduces three aligned taxonomies for [[Trust_Dimensions]] (e.g., fairness, transparency), [[Risk_Categories]] (e.g., model, data, legal), and [[Security_Controls]] (e.g., access management). Furthermore, it develops a detailed [[Toxicity_Taxonomy_for_Generative_AI]], mapping various harms (e.g., hate, misinformation, jailbreak attacks) to specific [[TRiSM_Safeguards]]. Through cross-domain case studies, it illustrates practical integration patterns. Finally, the paper identifies key adoption challenges and proposes a roadmap towards future advancements like [[AI-Driven_Policy_Engines]], [[Causal_Explainability]], and [[Quantum-Safe_Architectures]], aiming to guide practitioners in designing resilient and ethical AI systems.

### 2.2. Core Framework and Pillars

The paper synthesizes [[AI_TRiSM]] around **five foundational pillars**:

1.  **[[Explainability]] with [[Real-Time_Drift_Monitoring]]**: Ensuring transparency and interpretability of AI decisions, alongside continuous monitoring for performance degradation or concept drift.
2.  **[[ModelOps_Governance]]**: Establishing processes and controls for the entire [[Model_Lifecycle]], from development to deployment and maintenance.
3.  **[[Application-Level_Security]]**: Protecting AI applications from vulnerabilities and attacks at the software and service layer.
4.  **[[Data_Protection_and_Privacy]]**: Implementing measures to safeguard sensitive data used by AI systems, adhering to privacy regulations.
5.  **[[Adversarial_Resilience]]**: Building AI systems robust against malicious attacks designed to mislead or compromise them.

## 5. Stated Limitations and Identified Challenges

The paper identifies several key challenges in the adoption and implementation of [[AI_TRiSM]], which can be interpreted as current limitations of the field that the paper aims to address and guide future work on:

*   **Fragmented Tooling**: Lack of integrated tools and platforms for managing different aspects of [[TRiSM]].
*   **Late-Stage Governance**: Governance considerations are often introduced too late in the [[AI_Lifecycle]], making effective integration difficult.
*   **Scalability Constraints**: Challenges in scaling [[TRiSM]] solutions to enterprise-level AI deployments.
*   **Evolving Threats**: The dynamic and rapidly changing nature of threats against [[AI_Systems]].
*   **Evolving Regulations**: The continuous development and update of regulatory frameworks pertaining to AI.

## 6. Future Directions and Roadmap

The paper charts a forward-looking roadmap towards:

*   **Adaptive, [[AI-Driven_Policy_Engines]]**: Systems that can automatically adapt and enforce TRiSM policies.
*   **[[Causal_Explainability]]**: Advancing explainability beyond correlations to understand causal relationships in AI decisions.
*   **[[Privacy-by-Design_Pipelines]]**: Integrating privacy considerations from the initial design phase of AI systems.
*   **[[Continuous_Real-Time_Assurance]]**: Implementing continuous monitoring and validation for ongoing TRiSM compliance.
*   **[[Federated_Governance]]**: Distributed governance models for AI systems, particularly relevant for decentralized or federated learning scenarios.
*   **[[Quantum-Safe_Architectures]]**: Designing AI systems and their underlying infrastructure to be resilient against quantum computing threats.
*   **Sustainable "[[Green_AI]]" Practices**: Focusing on environmentally friendly and resource-efficient AI development and deployment.
