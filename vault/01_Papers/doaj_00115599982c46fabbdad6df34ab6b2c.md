# Perception of dental trauma by community health workers in the Amazon region

## Claims and Hypotheses

*   **Primary Aim:** To evaluate the [[Perception]] of [[Community Health Workers]] (CHW) in relation to [[Dental Trauma]].
*   **Specific Aim:** To evaluate the association between the [[Experience]] of the [[CHW]] and their respective [[Knowledge]] and [[Attitudes]] regarding [[Dentoalveolar Trauma]].
*   **Implicit Hypothesis:** The authors explore the hypothesis that there might be an association between CHW experience and their knowledge/attitudes towards dental trauma.

## Mathematical Formulas, Loss Functions, and Architecture Hyper-parameters

No explicit mathematical formulas, loss functions, or architecture hyper-parameters were presented in the paper. The statistical methods are described under [[Methodology]].

## Methodology and Algorithms

*   **Study Design:** A [[Cross-sectional Qualitative Study]] was conducted.
*   **Data Collection Instrument:** A [[Questionnaire]] was used to collect data.
*   **Evaluation Objective:** To assess the association between CHW experience and their knowledge and attitudes regarding [[Dental Trauma]].
*   **Statistical Analysis:**
    *   Data tabulation followed by statistical analysis.
    *   [[Chi-square Test]]
    *   [[Fisher's Exact Test]]
    *   **Significance Level (α):** α = 0.05

## Experimental Results, Datasets, Sample Sizes, and Quantitative Benchmarks

*   **Dataset/Sample Size:**
    *   **Population:** [[Community Health Workers]] (CHW).
    *   **Sample Size:** 169 CHW.
    *   **Geographic Location:** Metropolitan region of [[Belém]], in the state of [[Pará]], [[Brazil]] (Amazon region).
*   **Key Quantitative Benchmarks (Descriptive Analysis):**
    *   **Lack of Knowledge on Dental Avulsion:** 93.5% of CHW are unaware of what [[Dental Avulsion]] is.
    *   **Attitude towards Reimplantation:** 68% of CHW would not attempt to reimplant a tooth in cases of [[Dental Avulsion]].
    *   **Attitude towards Fragment Storage:** 53.84% of CHW would not store a dental fragment in any kind of solution after traumatism.
*   **Inferential Results:**
    *   The results indicated that there is **no association** between the [[Experience]] of the [[CHW]] and their respective [[Knowledge]] and [[Attitude]] concerning [[Dentoalveolar Trauma]].
*   **Overall Conclusion:** The perceptions of CHW on dentoalveolar trauma are insufficient, demonstrating an unsatisfactory performance and lack of basic knowledge on the topic.

The authors did not explicitly state any limitations of the study within the provided text.
