## [[Claims and Hypotheses]]

*   [[LLM-based agents]] excel at integrating multi-source knowledge into coherent reasoning chains.
*   These agents remain opaque and difficult to audit without embedded, in-loop [[AI safety mechanisms]].
*   Existing surveys treat reasoning, agentic behavior, and safety in isolation, leaving a gap in how to integrate them into practical, trustworthy agents.
*   The introduction of [[Responsible Reasoning AI Agents (R2A2)]] will enable [[LLM-based agents]] to generate explicit reasoning traces while enforcing [[Responsible AI Principles]] (fairness, privacy, transparency, accountability, and auditability) throughout the decision loop.
*   The proposed taxonomy, metric suite, and framework will advance the development of safe, transparent, and governable [[LLM-based agents]].

## [[Methodology and Architecture]]

This paper presents a critical survey and proposes a new class of agents, an evaluation framework, and an evaluation protocol.

### **Purpose**
To address a gap in existing research by integrating [[reasoning]], [[agentic behavior]], and [[AI safety]] into a unified approach for practical, trustworthy [[AI agents]].

### **Proposed System: Responsible Reasoning AI Agents (R2A2)**
*   **Definition**: A class of agentic [[LLM systems]] designed to generate explicit reasoning traces.
*   **Core Principle**: Enforces [[Responsible AI Principles]] (fairness, privacy, transparency, accountability, and auditability) throughout the decision loop.
*   **Synthesized Advances**: Integrates recent developments in:
    *   [[Chain-of-thought prompting]]
    *   [[ReAct]]
    *   [[Tree/Graph-of-thought structures]]
    *   [[Tool use]]
    *   [[Memory]]
    *   [[Retrieval]]
    *   [[Agentic browsing]]
*   **Framework**: These synthesized advances are integrated with [[Responsible AI Principles]] into a unified evaluation framework.

### **Proposed Evaluation Methodology**
*   **Focus**: Evaluating agentic reasoning with embedded safety mechanisms.
*   **Reproducible Protocol (Five-stage)**: Designed to operationalize responsibility metrics.
    1.  **Curate**: Implies the selection and preparation of relevant data.
    2.  **Unify**: Suggests standardizing data and representations.
    3.  **Probe**: Involves testing and querying the agent's behavior.
    4.  **Benchmark**: Refers to quantitative assessment against established criteria or baselines.
    5.  **Analyze**: Pertains to interpreting the results to understand performance and adherence to responsibility metrics.

### **Taxonomy and Metric Suite**
The paper proposes a new taxonomy and a suite of metrics as part of its framework to advance the development of governable [[LLM-based agents]].
