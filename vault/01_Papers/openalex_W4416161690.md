## [[Methodology and Architecture]]

The paper proposes a **five-layered framework** for leveraging [[Artificial Intelligence (AI)]] in strategic business decision-making:

### Framework Layers:

1.  **[[Problem Framing and Value Hypotheses]]**:
    *   **Objective**: Decompose strategic questions into actionable components.
    *   **Components**:
        *   [[Decision Statements]]
        *   [[Testable Hypotheses]]
        *   [[Measurable Value Drivers]]
    *   **Tools**: [[Logic Models]], [[Decision Trees]] to link choices to outcomes.

2.  **[[Data Readiness and Governance]]**:
    *   **Objective**: Establish a robust foundation for trustworthy and auditable data use.
    *   **Data Readiness Standards**:
        *   Quality
        *   Lineage
        *   Privacy
        *   Security
    *   **Supporting Mechanisms**:
        *   [[Metadata Catalogs]]
        *   [[Access Controls]]
        *   [[Data Stewardship Roles]]
    *   **Governance Alignment**:
        *   [[Regulatory Obligations]]
        *   [[Ethical Principles]]

3.  **[[Model Portfolio Design]]**:
    *   **Objective**: Blend diverse AI techniques for strategic insights.
    *   **Model Types**:
        *   **[[Predictive Models]]**: Quantify demand, risk, customer propensity (e.g., forecasting, uplift models).
        *   **[[Prescriptive Models]]**: Allocate resources under constraints (e.g., optimization, simulation).
        *   **[[Causal Inference Models]]**: Estimate treatment effects for policy and pricing (e.g., experimentation).
        *   **[[Generative AI]]**: Accelerate knowledge discovery, scenario authoring, decision briefings.
    *   **[[MLOps Backbone]] Orchestration**:
        *   [[Feature Stores]]
        *   Automated Pipelines
        *   Testing
        *   Deployment
        *   Monitoring (for reliability, fairness, performance)

4.  **[[Human–AI Teaming and Controls]]**:
    *   **Objective**: Integrate algorithmic recommendations with expert judgment.
    *   **Human–AI Teaming Mechanisms**:
        *   [[Decision Playbooks]]
        *   [[Role Clarity]]
        *   [[Calibrated Trust]]
    *   **Capabilities Enabled**: Interrogation, sensitivity analysis, override functionality.
    *   **Controls Integration (Model Risk Management with FATE)**:
        *   [[Fairness, Accountability, Transparency, and Ethics (FATE)]] Requirements
        *   Pre-deployment testing
        *   Bias and drift monitoring
        *   Audit-ready documentation
        *   Incident response procedures

5.  **[[Impact Measurement and Continuous Learning]]**:
    *   **Objective**: Connect decisions to financial and non-financial outcomes.
    *   **Measurement Techniques**:
        *   [[Causal Evaluation]]
        *   [[Objectives and Key Results (OKRs)]]
        *   [[Benefit Tracking]]
