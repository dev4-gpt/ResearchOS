# [[A Comprehensive Introspection on AI Risks: Taxonomy, Challenges and Future Directions]]

## Abstract Summary

This paper addresses the inadequacy of current AI risk frameworks, which were designed for static predictive models, in handling the dynamic, tool-driven failures of [[Autonomous Agents]] and [[Foundation Models]]. It introduces a unified [[Five-domain Taxonomy of AI Risks]]—comprising [[Agentic operation]], [[Training-data risks]], [[Inference behavior risks]], [[Output risks]], and [[Non-technological factors]]—inspired by the [[IBM AI risk atlas]]. Each domain is further classified by traditional vulnerabilities, risks amplified by generative capabilities, and novel hazards unique to generative agents. Through detailed case studies (e.g., [[Function-calling hallucinations]], [[Dynamic bias amplification]], [[Privacy leakage across toolchains]], [[Edge-deployment constraints]]), the paper examines failure modes. It characterizes ten core operational challenges, recommends mitigation strategies, and proposes twelve future research and engineering directions (e.g., [[Adaptive fairness loops]], [[Federated privacy collaboration]], [[Schema-guided invocation]], [[Policy-as-code governance]], [[Green AI practices]]) to foster resilient and sustainable agentic systems.

## Research Claims & Contributions

The core contributions of this article include:
1.  **Unified Five-Domain Taxonomy**: Introduction of a new, comprehensive taxonomy for AI risks specifically designed for [[Autonomous Agents]] and [[Foundation Models]].
2.  **Risk Classification**: Detailed classification of risks within each domain, distinguishing between traditional vulnerabilities, those amplified by generative capabilities, and novel hazards.
3.  **Detailed Case Studies**: Examination of how specific failure modes (e.g., [[Function-calling hallucinations]], [[Dynamic bias amplification]]) arise, interact, and propagate across multi-stage AI pipelines.
4.  **Operational Challenges**: Characterization of ten core operational challenges in managing AI risks.
5.  **Mitigation Strategies**: Recommendation of targeted monitoring and mitigation strategies for identified challenges.
6.  **Future Directions**: Proposal of twelve future research and engineering directions to guide the development of resilient, transparent, and sustainable agentic systems.
7.  **Structured Foundation**: Providing a structured foundation for systematic risk assessment, targeted intervention, and continual oversight for various stakeholders.
