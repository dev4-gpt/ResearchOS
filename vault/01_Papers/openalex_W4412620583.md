## Claims and Hypotheses

*   **Claim 1**: Existing [[AI Risk Assessment Frameworks]], developed for static predictive models, struggle to address the dynamic, tool-driven failures emerging from [[Autonomous Agents]] and [[Foundation Models]].
*   **Claim 2**: Unanticipated harms (e.g., biased decision logic, privacy breaches, adversarial exploits, unsustainable resource consumption) highlight a critical need for an [[AI Risk]] methodology that spans end-to-end, multi-stage pipelines.
*   **Hypothesis**: A unified five-domain taxonomy of risks, coupled with an analysis of core operational challenges and future research directions, can provide a structured foundation for systematic [[AI Risk Assessment]], targeted intervention, and continual oversight.
