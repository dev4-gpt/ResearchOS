## Introduction
The rise of digital transformation has posed significant challenges on organizations, necessitating the use of data in decision-making to gain a competitive edge. Conventional business intelligence solutions struggle to manage the enormous amount, speed, and diversity of data produced by business organizations today, leading to delayed insights and loss of opportunities.

## Methodology
The presented research applies a systematic review based on the [[PRISMA Methodology]] to explore the opportunity of integrating [[Artificial Intelligence]], [[Big Data Analytics]], [[Machine Learning]], [[Internet of Things]], and [[Blockchain]] technologies in transforming business intelligence systems.
