## Abstract Summary

This literature review addresses the challenges conventional [[Business Intelligence]] solutions face in managing the velocity, volume, and variety of modern business data. It provides an analysis of the intersection of [[Artificial Intelligence]] (AI), [[Big Data Analytics]], [[Machine Learning]] (ML), [[Internet of Things]] (IoT), and [[Blockchain]] technologies in transforming [[Business Intelligence]] systems. The research employs a [[PRISMA Methodology]]-based systematic review to explore how these technologies can collaboratively enhance [[Predictive Analytics]], automate business processes, ensure information integrity, and create business value. The review examines implementation structures, technical architecture models, and algorithm types across applications such as supply chain optimization, customer experience, fraud detection, and strategic planning. Key conclusions highlight that integrated AI-ML-Big Data environments improve predictive quality, [[IoT]] provides real-time operational intelligence, and [[Blockchain]] assures data provenance and trust. However, significant challenges persist, including data quality, integration complexity, skills gaps, ethical concerns, and regulatory compliance.

## [[Claims and Hypotheses]]

The authors make the following explicit claims based on their literature review:

*   Integrated [[Artificial Intelligence]] (AI)-[[Machine Learning]] (ML)-[[Big Data Analytics]] environments will provide better [[predictive quality]].
*   [[Internet of Things]] (IoT) allows acquiring real-time operational intelligence.
*   [[Blockchain]] guarantees data provenance and trust.
*   The combination of these technologies can enhance [[Predictive Analytics]], automate business processes, guarantee information integrity, and generate unprecedented business value.
