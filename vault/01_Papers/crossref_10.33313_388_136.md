# Novel Digital Solutions to Optimize OPEX and Quality for Galvanizing Furnaces

**Authors**: Sébastien Roche, Maxime Monnoyer
**Published**: 2024-5-25 | **Citations**: 1 | **Source**: Crossref
**URL**: https://doi.org/10.33313/388/136

## Executive Summary & Abstract
Through its Steel Digital Factory, Fives has developed a wide range of digital solutions dedicated to OPEX improvements, quality enhancement and productivity optimization. This paper will give technical descriptions for Fives’ main digital solutions and provide case studies for each solution:
                  Real-time quality management system tracing all process steps, quality events and product information from the melt shop to finishing lines. This solution is performing automatic product grading, providing recommendations for product reallocation and repair and allows defect, process &amp; equipment analysis. It will be depicted in detail in a dedicated paper. Thermal optimizer for reheating furnaces utilizing advanced modelling and dynamic control to ensure heating efficiency. This solution demonstrates an improvement of process stability, energy consumption, scale losses, emissions and output. Furnace process optimizer for strip processing lines providing optimized control of annealing furnaces to achieve targeted mechanical properties through precise transition management and reducing energy consumption. This solution controls furnace temperature with high accuracy and optimizes line speed based on physical models and AI with predictive control modeling (MPC) philosophy. Comprehensive solution for fully automatic control of the process sections in annealing lines using metallurgical and process predictive models, including Furnace process optimizer. This solution optimizes process operations to produce each coil as a first choice from head to tail end. All key processes (furnace, Zn bath and Air knife) are connected and managed, and incoming coil data is processed to predict individual setpoints for targeted product properties.
