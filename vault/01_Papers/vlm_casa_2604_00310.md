The version-2 primary abstract describes an internal safety-token decision before generation, using attention over a shared multimodal representation. It reports text-trained safety transfer to image/audio and evaluates the contribution of a standard safety prompt separately from the internal mechanism. This review verified the version-2 abstract only; the method and results have not undergone full-text audit.
