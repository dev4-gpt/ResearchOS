## Abstract

This document serves as a summary and introduction to a panel discussion concerning the enduring relevance of [[Analog Test]] for [[Mixed-Signal Circuits]] and [[RF Circuits]]. The field has been an active research topic for over 25 years, continuously attracting submissions to major test conferences. The panel aims to explore the reasons behind this longevity – whether it indicates a lack of progress or a domain in constant evolution facing new challenges that necessitate novel ideas. Experts from both academia and industry will present their arguments and views on the future of this topic.

## Key Claims and Observations

*   **Longevity of Research:** The test of [[Analog]], [[Mixed-Signal Circuits]], and [[RF Circuits]] has been a topic of active research for more than 25 years.
*   **Continued Relevance:** Despite extensive research, it remains a "hot topic" attracting significant submissions to major test conferences.
*   **Underlying Question:** The persistent activity raises a fundamental question: does it mean that previously proposed ideas are "useless," or is the domain constantly evolving with new challenges demanding novel solutions?
