The version 2 arXiv abstract reports that attaching a blank image can change refusal on matched prompts, especially for borderline-benign cases, with effects depending on image properties and checkpoint. It separately compares effects on benign refusal and attack success. This makes a generic blank-image refusal effect or safety/utility cost an existing result to replicate or extend, not an available novelty claim for this project. The abstract alone does not establish the paper's detailed methods or reproducibility.
