The abstract introduces safety calibration over both unsafe compliance and refusal of safe inputs, using 3,600 image-text pairs with similar content but different safety labels and evaluating eleven VLMs. It reports that some calibration methods cost utility. Therefore, over-refusal and safe usefulness need explicit measurement in this project; general safety-versus-utility tension is prior work.
