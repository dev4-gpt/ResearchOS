# How generative AI is reshaping UI/UX design workflows: A systematic review

## Introduction

This paper presents a [[Systematic Literature Review]] focusing on the integration and implications of [[Generative AI]] ([[GenAI]]) technologies, such as [[Large Language Models]], [[Diffusion Models]], and [[Multimodal Generative Systems]], within [[UI/UX Design]] workflows. The review aims to synthesize current knowledge, identify key trends, and pinpoint existing research gaps.

## Research Questions

The review is structured around five specific research questions (RQs):

1.  What are the stages of the [[UI/UX Design Process]] where [[GenAI]] tools are most actively applied?
2.  What are the methodological approaches used to evaluate the integration of [[GenAI]] in [[UI/UX Design]]?
3.  What are the ethical considerations arising from the use of [[GenAI]] in design?
4.  What models of [[Human-AI Collaboration]] exist in design practice?
5.  What are the research gaps that shape the future trajectory of this field?

## Methodology

This study employed a [[Systematic Literature Review]] approach.

*   **Corpus**: The review drew upon N=50 [[peer-reviewed articles]] and [[preprint articles]].
*   **Publication Window**: Articles were selected from those published between 2020 and 2025.
*   **Focus**: The papers specifically explored [[Generative AI]] in [[User Interface (UI)]] and [[User Experience (UX)]] design contexts.

## Key Findings

The findings are structured according to the research questions:

### RQ1: Stages of GenAI Application in UI/UX Design

*   [[GenAI]] tools are "widely adopted" in:
    *   [[Prototyping]]
    *   [[Visual Asset Generation]]
*   Usage of [[GenAI]] tools remains "limited" in:
    *   [[Early-stage Conceptualization]]
    *   [[UX Evaluation]]

### RQ2: Methodological Approaches for Evaluation

*   The literature reveals "methodological fragmentation".
*   There is a "lack of standardized evaluation frameworks" for [[GenAI]] integration in design.

### RQ3: Ethical Considerations

*   Ethical concerns are "underexplored", particularly regarding:
    *   [[Bias]]
    *   [[Transparency]]
    *   [[Privacy]]

### RQ4: Models of Human-AI Collaboration

*   "Few studies provide robust models for collaborative work between humans and AI."

### RQ5: Research Gaps and Future Trajectory

The review identified several critical research gaps and needs:

*   The need for [[Longitudinal Research]].
*   The need for "structured participatory frameworks".
*   The need for "ethically grounded design methodologies".

## Limitations

The authors implicitly highlight the following areas as limitations of the current research landscape, which also serve as gaps for future work:

*   Limited application of [[GenAI]] in [[early-stage conceptualization]] and [[UX evaluation]].
*   Lack of [[standardized evaluation frameworks]] for [[GenAI]] in [[UI/UX Design]].
*   Underexplored ethical concerns such as [[bias]], [[transparency]], and [[privacy]].
*   Paucity of robust models for [[Human-AI Collaboration]].
