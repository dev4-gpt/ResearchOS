## Summary of Multidisciplinary Perspectives on Generative AI

This opinion paper compiles and synthesizes insights from 43 experts across diverse fields to explore the opportunities, challenges, and implications of [[Generative Conversational AI]] like [[ChatGPT]] for research, practice, and policy.

### Core Findings and Contributions

The paper presents a balanced view on [[ChatGPT]] by acknowledging its capabilities while also highlighting its limitations and potential negative impacts.

#### Identified Opportunities of Generative AI/ChatGPT:
*   **Productivity Enhancement**: [[ChatGPT]] has capabilities to significantly enhance productivity.
*   **Industry Gains**: Expected to offer significant gains in the following industries:
    *   [[Banking]]
    *   [[Hospitality and Tourism]]
    *   [[Information Technology]]
*   **Business Activity Enhancement**: Can enhance business activities such as [[Management]] and [[Marketing]].

#### Identified Challenges and Concerns of Generative AI/ChatGPT:
*   **Ethical and Legal Issues**: Significant ethical and legal challenges are presented by the technology.
*   **Privacy and Security**: Threats to privacy and security.
*   **Biases**: Consequences of biases attributable to training datasets and processes.
*   **Misuse and Misinformation**: Potential for misuse and the spread of misinformation.
*   **Disruptions**: Disruptions to existing practices.

#### Policy Stance:
*   Opinion is split among experts on whether the use of [[ChatGPT]] should be restricted or legislated.

### Research Questions and Avenues for Future Research

The article identifies questions requiring further research across three thematic areas:

1.  **Knowledge, Transparency, and Ethics**:
    *   Identifying skills, resources, and capabilities needed to handle [[Generative AI]].
    *   Examining biases of [[Generative AI]] attributable to training datasets and processes.
    *   Identifying ways to assess the accuracy of text produced by [[Generative AI]].
    *   Uncovering the ethical and legal issues in using [[Generative AI]] across different contexts.

2.  **Digital Transformation of Organisations and Societies**:
    *   Exploring business and societal contexts best suited for [[Generative AI]] implementation.
    *   Determining optimal combinations of human and [[Generative AI]] for various tasks.

3.  **Teaching, Learning, and Scholarly Research**:
    *   Specific avenues for research in this area are implicitly covered by the broader questions regarding skills, accuracy, and ethical/legal issues in academic contexts.
