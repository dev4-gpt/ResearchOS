# GenAI-Powered Safety and Permit-to-Work Automation Integrated with SAP Enterprise Asset Management

**Authors**: Monika Nanjappa
**Published**: 2026-7-29 | **Citations**: 0 | **Source**: Crossref
**URL**: https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.6855338

## Executive Summary & Abstract
Permit-to-work systems are critical administrative controls for hazardous maintenance and field execution activities in asset-intensive industries. Many permit processes remain dependent on manual review, fragmented asset context, and inconsistent retrieval of safety procedures. Enterprise asset management systems, including SAP EAM, contain structured information about technical objects, functional locations, maintenance orders, task lists, historical failures, and work execution status. This review examines how generative artificial intelligence and retrieval-augmented generation can support safety and permit-to-work automation when grounded in enterprise asset data and controlled safety knowledge. The paper synthesizes literature on large language models for safety assessment, electronic permit-to-work systems, occupational safety analytics, predictive maintenance, and Industry 4.0 asset management. The review finds that GenAI can assist with hazard identification, procedure retrieval, permit drafting, risk-control recommendation, and lessons-learned summarization. However, current research also shows important limitations involving reliability, explainability, validation, accountability, and measurable safety outcomes. The paper proposes a human-in-the-loop SAFE-EAM framework that connects SAP EAM data, approved safety procedures, isolation controls, predictive maintenance signals, and auditable GenAI outputs. The contribution is a practical taxonomy, comparison matrix, research gap matrix, maturity model, and implementation roadmap for high-hazard industries such as oil and gas, utilities, chemicals, mining, and heavy manufacturing.
