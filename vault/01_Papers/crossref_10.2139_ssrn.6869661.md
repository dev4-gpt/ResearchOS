## [[System Architecture and Components]]

The paper discusses the following architectural components and concepts in the context of enterprise AI:

*   **[[Generative AI]]**: General term for AI systems capable of generating new content.
*   **[[Agentic Ecosystems]] / [[Agentic Architectures]]**: AI systems characterized by multi-step reasoning and autonomous tool-use capabilities. These are highlighted as introducing new vulnerabilities.
*   **[[Tier-1 Productivity Suites]]**: Commercial software suites offering productivity tools, identified as a driver for generative AI adoption.
*   **[[Enterprise Infrastructure APIs]]**: Application Programming Interfaces that facilitate integration of AI capabilities into existing enterprise infrastructure.
*   **[[Zero-Trust AI Architectures]]**: A proposed target state for enterprise AI systems, emphasizing continuous verification and minimal privilege, designed to be auditable.
*   **[[Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)]]**: A technique for improving AI generation by retrieving relevant information from a corpus, which is susceptible to corpus poisoning.
