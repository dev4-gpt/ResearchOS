# Building an intelligent brain platform for small and medium-sized enterprises using ChatGLM and Multi-Agent Systems

## 1. Introduction

This study addresses the challenges of directly applying [[Large Language Models (LLMs)]] in specialized [[Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (SMEs)]] domains, which include semantic overgeneralization, poor alignment with enterprise-specific knowledge, and insufficient domain expertise. It proposes an "**Enterprise Intelligent Brain**" platform to overcome these limitations, specifically tailored for SMEs. The platform leverages [[ChatGLM]], [[Multi-Agent Systems]], and [[Enterprise Knowledge Graphs]] to enhance semantic adaptability and intelligent responsiveness in real-world enterprise scenarios.

## 2. Claims & Hypotheses

*   **Claim**: Direct application of [[LLMs]] in segmented and specialized domains of [[SMEs]] presents challenges such as semantic overgeneralization, poor alignment with enterprise-specific knowledge, and insufficient domain expertise.
*   **Hypothesis**: An "Enterprise Intelligent Brain" platform built upon [[ChatGLM]], enhanced through a [[multi-agent coordination mechanism]] and structured support from [[enterprise knowledge graphs]], can improve semantic adaptability and intelligent responsiveness for [[SMEs]].
*   **Claim**: The proposed optimized system will significantly outperform baseline models across multiple metrics in [[SME]] contexts.

## 3. Methodology & Architecture

### 3.1. Core Platform Design

The proposed system is an "[[Enterprise Intelligent Brain]]" platform designed for [[SMEs]]. It is built upon the [[Chat Global Language Model (ChatGLM)]] and enhanced by:
*   A [[multi-agent coordination mechanism]].
*   Structured support from [[enterprise knowledge graphs]].

The platform focuses on improving semantic adaptability and intelligent responsiveness for typical [[SME]] operations, such as policy consultation, customer service, and business process execution.

### 3.2. Triadic System Architecture

The platform integrates three key components:
1.  **Semantic Parsing**: Responsible for understanding the meaning of user queries and enterprise-specific texts.
2.  **Task Scheduling**: Manages and allocates tasks to appropriate agents or processes based on parsed semantics.
3.  **Knowledge Support**: Provides relevant domain expertise and contextual information, primarily through [[enterprise knowledge graphs]].

### 3.3. Methodological Enhancements

*   **Domain-Specific Fine-tuning**: [[ChatGLM]] undergoes domain-specific fine-tuning to enhance its relevance and precision within [[SME]] contexts.
*   **Multi-Agent Task Allocation Framework**: Incorporated to improve the efficiency and accuracy of task distribution and execution.
*   **Knowledge Graph Reasoning**: Utilized to improve contextual accuracy and integrate domain knowledge effectively, overcoming issues like semantic overgeneralization and poor knowledge alignment.
