MARS studies transfer of textual refusal directions to image/video inputs. Its method combines activation re-centering, adaptive intervention strength, and layer selection. The authors report cross-modal safety improvements and utility preservation, while acknowledging residual over-refusal and assumptions about backbone refusal structure and approximately linear modality correction. Full text inspected: introduction, method framing, conclusion and limitations.

Proposal implication (our interpretation): shared cross-modal steering is established prior work. A simple text-derived refusal vector or an increase in image refusal is insufficient novelty. The reported results motivate testing joint improvement as a competing hypothesis to a universal text/image seesaw; they do not settle our models or protocol.
